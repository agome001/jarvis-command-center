# J.A.R.V.I.S. Command Center

Universal AI business operations and ACE Forex demo-trading oversight.

## Planned modules
- Executive dashboard and AI command interface
- Universal Business Builder for any legal business: formation, filings, licensing, insurance, compliance and launch
- Multi-company operations, projects, accounting and finance
- Business Opportunity Radar
- ACE Forex integration with human-approved demo trades and server-enforced risk controls

## Safety principles
- Require explicit authorization for government submissions, payments and trades.
- No live trading in the initial release.
- Keep credentials out of source control.
- Isolate company data with authenticated access and database policies.
- Distinguish drafted, submitted and approved filings.

## Development roadmap
1. Next.js, TypeScript and Tailwind application foundation
2. Supabase authentication and multi-company database
3. Dashboard and AI command interface
4. Business Builder and compliance workflows
5. Opportunity research and operations
6. ACE Forex secure integration and approval workflows
